# Roadmap

## Open decisions
- Pick one control scheme to focus on: FIFA layout, Mouse aim or Player cam.
- Should AI teammates refuse passes when you are marked, or sometimes force them?
- Player builds: attributes and PlayStyle-like abilities (from the EA FC Clubs reference).

## Training ground (now)
- [x] Finishing, goalkeeping, defending, passing drills
- [x] Match play: calling for the ball, positioning marker, offside, one-twos
- [x] Beginner / Pro modes, three control schemes, gamepad and touch
- [x] Real goal net, shot aim reticle
- [ ] Player builds and archetypes
- [ ] More set pieces (free kicks, corners, penalties)

## Platform (next)
- [ ] Accounts and server-side profiles (replace localStorage)
- [ ] Clubs: buying with points or LagosLife points, signing players and coaches, squads of about 20
- [ ] Online 5-a-side matches (needs authoritative servers; likely a Unity or dedicated netcode build)
- [ ] Spectator mode with broadcast cameras
- [ ] In-app points sportsbook
- [ ] Friendlies, league, Champions League, forfeits with heavy points deductions
- [ ] AI governance commissions (integrity reports, match-fixing checks), human review later
- [ ] Car racing

## Coming soon (not before licensing)
